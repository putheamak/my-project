## How to answer (videos 2, 3, …)
1. Every comment: reply in text within a few hours ("អរគុណ! យើងកំពុងគណនា 🙏") — fast replies keep reach up.
2. Pick 1–3 routes per day. On a phone, screen-record khmerparcel.com calculating that route (30 fps, 10–20 s).
3. Send the recording here → I add "@Name សួរ៖ A → B", the real km/price from the recording, music and end card.
4. Reply to the person's comment with the new video link; they usually share it.
5. Write down every route asked (Khan → Khan) — that list is your demand map and price feedback.

Rules: only show prices the website itself calculates; ask before showing a commenter's name or photo
(or use first name only).
