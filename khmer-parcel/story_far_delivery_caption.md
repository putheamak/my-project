## English
**2 parcels · $2 · 3 hours — profit or loss?**

One day we got a booking to pick up in **Chbar Ampov** and deliver to **Choam Chao** — from one edge of Phnom Penh to the other.
Just 2 parcels, $2 in total. Our rider turned it down because it was too far.

So I rode it myself. Pick-up to delivery took almost 3 hours.
The maths: $2 ÷ 3 hours ≈ $0.67 an hour, before fuel. We lost money, but we earned trust.

**What we learned:**
• Riders: few parcels or many, deliver with a smile — every parcel is a customer's trust.
• Online sellers: a fair delivery fee values everyone's work — seller, rider and customer all win.

Far or near, every parcel matters.
