# រកអ្នកលក់អនឡាញនៅឯណា? · Where to find online sellers (Nov 2026)

Goal: reach **10 sellers per day** (messages) + **15 flyers per day** (market visit).
Use the first message in `../seller_outreach_kit.md` + the 10 s video `../khmer_parcel_pitch_seller_10s.mp4`.
Write every contact in the tracking sheet (section 5 of the outreach kit).

> Search words below are suggestions, not group names. Groups change all the time — type the words, open the
> groups with many members and posts **today**, and only post where the group rules allow it.

---

Type in Facebook search → tab **Groups / ក្រុម**:

**What to do in a group**
1. Do **not** spam the group wall. Look at today's posts.
2. A post with photos + price + "inbox" / "ឆាត" = a seller. Open their page → **Message** them (not a comment).
3. Only post Khmer Parcel in a group if the rules allow ads. Then post the flyer image + one short line, max once a week per group.

## 2. Facebook — pages and Live sellers

Facebook search → tab **Pages** or **Videos/Live**:
- `ហាង` + what they sell: `ហាងសម្លៀកបំពាក់`, `ហាងគ្រឿងសំអាង`, `ហាងស្បែកជើង`
- `Live លក់`, `លក់ផ្ទាល់`, `Live sale Phnom Penh`

**Live sellers are the best target**: they sell many orders in one night and need delivery the next day.
Message them the day after their Live.

## 3. TikTok shops

TikTok search (videos and accounts):
- `#លក់អនឡាញ` `#ហាងអនឡាញ` `#ភ្នំពេញ` `#onlineshopcambodia` `#tiktokshopcambodia`
- `#សម្លៀកបំពាក់` `#គ្រឿងសំអាង` `#រកលុយអនឡាញ`

Pick accounts that post every day and write "ដឹកជូនដល់ផ្ទះ" / "COD". Send a DM, or comment once politely
("ហាងបងស្អាតណាស់ 👍 ខ្មែរផាសែល ដឹកក្នុងភ្នំពេញ $0.75 ប្រមូល COD ជូន — ឆាតមកបាន").

## 4. Telegram

Telegram search (top bar): `ហាង`, `shop`, `លក់`, `ទិញលក់`, `Phnom Penh shop`.
Many sellers have a **channel** with a contact link in the description → message that contact.
Do not add people to groups without asking.

| ផ្សារអូរឫស្សី · Orussey | ប្រាំពីរមករា | wholesale, cosmetics, many online sellers |
| ផ្សារទួលទំពូង · Russian Market | ចំការមន | clothes, souvenirs, spare parts |
| ផ្សារអូឡាំពិក · Olympic Market | ប្រាំពីរមករា | clothes, fabric, wholesale |
| ផ្សារដើមគរ · Doeum Kor | ទួលគោក | food, household, mixed |
| ផ្សារកណ្ដាល · Kandal Market | ដូនពេញ | clothes, food |

Ask at the stall: **«បងលក់អនឡាញដែរទេ?»** — if yes, say the 20-second pitch (section 4 of the outreach kit), leave the flyer,
write down name + Telegram. Best time: 2–4 pm (quiet hours).

## 6. Your own network (free and fastest)
- Post the pitch video in your **personal** Facebook and Telegram (not the Page) — friends know sellers.
- Ask every person who received a parcel: «បងស្គាល់អ្នកលក់អនឡាញទេ?»
- Stick the QR sticker (`../print/khmer_parcel_qr_stickers_A4.pdf`) on every parcel.
