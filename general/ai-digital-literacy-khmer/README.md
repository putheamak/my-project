# Khmer AI Literacy Lessons

First draft (October 2026) of six short AI lessons for Cambodians with low digital literacy:
farmers, market sellers, older people and anyone new to smartphones. Khmer first, short English.
Follows `general/ai-in-the-future-of-cambodia/`.

`ai_lessons_khmer.html` is the lesson page, with a ខ្មែរ + EN / ខ្មែរ / English toggle and tap-to-answer quizzes.
Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/RHEe8sm6AUDX1f1Hchvhoj

Each lesson has a goal, a remember sentence, a short explanation, a local story, a practice task and three quiz questions.
The page also includes trainer notes: 30–45 minutes per lesson, groups of 10–20, one phone with a speaker, practice at the end.

Before using in class: have a native Khmer speaker review the text (Gemini can also do a second review).
Owner-confirmed terms used in this series: UBI = ប្រាក់ចំណូលគោលជាសកល · IDPoor = ប័ណ្ណក្រីក្រ · NSSF = ប.ស.ស
