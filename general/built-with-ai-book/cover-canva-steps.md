# Making the cover in Canva: step by step

For *Built with AI, No Coding*. Follows the cover brief in `launch-kit-draft.md`. About 45–60 minutes.

## Before you start
- Use a computer if possible (canva.com in a browser); the phone app works too, but small text is harder to place.
- A free Canva account is enough. Use free elements only, or check the licence of any paid element.

## Step 1: Create the right size
1. On the Canva home page, click **Create a design** → **Custom size**.
2. Width **1600**, height **2560**, unit **px**. Click **Create new design**.

## Step 2: Background colour
1. Click the empty page, then the **colour square** in the top toolbar.
2. Click **+** (new colour) and type **#12355B** (deep blue).

## Step 3: Title
1. **Text** → **Add a heading**. Type: **BUILT WITH AI,** (Enter) **NO CODING**
2. Font: **Montserrat**, style **Extra Bold** (or **Poppins Bold**). Colour **white** (#FFFFFF).
3. Size about **190–220**. Centre it (alignment button). Drag the box to the upper third of the page, leaving space at the top.
4. Make **NO CODING** orange: select only those words and set the colour to **#F08A14**.

## Step 4: Cover line (short subtitle)
1. **Text** → **Add a subheading**. Type: *Real business tools, real costs, real mistakes*
2. Font: **Open Sans** (or **Montserrat Regular**), size about **70**, colour white, centred, just under the title.

## Step 5: The four icons
1. **Elements** → search, one at a time: **"form icon"**, **"blog icon"** (or "article icon"), **"play button icon"**, **"book icon"**.
2. Choose simple **line icons** in the same style (filter: *Graphics*, free). Recolour each to white.
3. Make each about **260 × 260 px**. Place the four in a row, or a 2 × 2 square, in the middle of the cover, evenly spaced (Canva shows pink guide lines when they line up).
4. Optional: **Elements** → **Lines & shapes** → a thin orange line or arrows joining the icons, like steps.
5. Do **not** use logos of Telegram, Claude, Amazon or any other company.

## Step 6: Author name
1. **Text** → **Add a subheading**. Type: **MAK PUTHEA**
2. Font **Montserrat Bold**, size about **90**, colour white, letter spacing about **200** (the "Spacing" button). Centre it near the bottom, about 150 px above the edge.

## Step 7: Check balance
- Top third: title. Middle: icons. Bottom: author name. Plenty of empty blue space around each.
- Zoom out to **10–15%**: this is roughly how big the cover looks as an Amazon thumbnail. If you can still read **BUILT WITH AI, NO CODING**, it works. If not, make the title bigger or the subtitle smaller.

## Step 8: Download
1. **Share** → **Download**. File type **JPG**, quality **maximum (100)**, size **1600 × 2560** (do not reduce).
2. Click **Download**. Rename the file `cover.jpg`.

## Step 9: Send it to me
Upload `cover.jpg` in this chat. I will check it against KDP's requirements (size, colour mode, legibility) and give you feedback before you upload it to KDP.

## KDP cover rules to remember
- JPG or TIFF, RGB colour, ideally 1600 × 2560 px.
- No price, no "Kindle" or "Amazon" words, no other brands' logos on the cover.
- The title and author name on the cover must match the details you enter in KDP.
