# Introduction

I am not a programmer. I have never studied computer science. Until recently, I had never built an app, run a website or edited a video.

Yet in the space of a few weeks, working from Phnom Penh, I built four real tools for my work:

- a **booking app** inside Telegram, so that online sellers can book deliveries with my parcel company in seconds
- a **blog that drafts its own articles** three times a week, which I review and approve before anything is published
- a **video ad factory** that turns simple designs into short advertisements in Khmer and English
- an **ebook system** that helped me plan, research and write the book you are reading now

All four were built in conversation with an AI. I described what I needed in plain words, the AI built it, I tested it, and together we fixed what went wrong. The tools behind all four projects cost me about $31 a month.

This book tells that story, honestly, from the beginning.

### Why I wrote it

There are already many books about AI. Most of them promise something: easy money, a hundred clever prompts, a business that runs while you sleep. When I looked at them on Amazon, I noticed something missing. Very few were written by an ordinary person showing real projects, with real costs and real mistakes.

That is what I can offer. I am a former research supervisor who left a difficult job, started a small delivery business, and decided to learn AI by using it every day. I am still learning. Some of my projects have only just begun, and I will tell you exactly where each one stands, including the parts that have not worked yet.

### Who this book is for

This book is for you if:

- you run a small business, or would like to start one, and you have never written code
- you have heard that AI can do amazing things, but you are not sure where to begin
- you want to understand AI's risks as well as its promise
- English is not your first language, and you have wondered whether AI is really for people like you

This book is probably not for you if you are looking for a quick way to get rich, or if you are already an experienced software developer. You will find no income promises here, and no complicated code.

### What you will find inside

**Part One, Why I Started,** tells my own story: the job that taught me why data and honest decisions matter, the warnings now coming from inside the AI industry, the growing divide between those who use AI and those who are left behind, and the reasons I chose to build four real projects.

**Part Two, Building,** is the heart of the book. It shows what my tools cost, how I learned to talk to AI so that it builds what I actually need, and how you can build your own first small tool in about thirty minutes. Then it tells the story of each of my four projects: the problem, the decisions, the reasons behind them, what went wrong, and what happened next.

**Part Three, What Comes Next,** gathers the hardest lessons I learned, about AI's mistakes, about keeping your business and your customers safe, and about the real cost of building, which is time. It ends with a simple seven-day plan to help you start your own first project.

### How to read this book

Each chapter in Part Two tells a story first, and explains the reasons behind each decision. If you want to build something yourself, look for the boxes called **Builder's Notes** at the end of the chapters. They contain the practical steps, the messages you can send to an AI, and the traps to avoid. If you only want the ideas, you can skip them without missing the story.

You do not need to read the book in order. If you are eager to start, Chapters 6 and 7 will get you building quickly. But I hope you will also read the story around them, because the "why" behind each decision is often more useful than the "how".

### A promise, and a confession

I made myself one promise while writing this book: **nothing in it would be invented.** Every result, every number and every story is real. Where a result does not exist yet, I say so. Where I quote someone, I have checked the source. Where I was wrong, I tell you.

And now the confession. This book was written with the help of AI, the same way my four projects were built. I shared my notes, my memories and my opinions, sometimes in imperfect English and sometimes mixed with Khmer. The AI helped me turn them into clear chapters, checked facts and sources, and told me when something could not be verified. Then I read every chapter, corrected it and rewrote it until it said what I meant. The experience, the decisions and the mistakes are all mine. In Chapter 11, I explain exactly how we worked together.

I think that is fitting. This is a book about what one person can build with AI, and the book itself is one of those things.

### Before we begin

When I started this journey, I asked myself a simple question: *if AI is going to change the way we all live and work, am I ready?* At the beginning, my honest answer was no. Today, I am much more ready than I was, not because I understand everything, but because I have started.

I hope this book helps you start too.
