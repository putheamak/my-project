# Introduction

I am not a programmer. I have never studied computer science. Until recently, I had never built an app, run a website or edited a video.

Yet within a few weeks, working from Phnom Penh, I built four working tools for my business and my writing:

- a **booking app** inside Telegram, which allows online sellers to book deliveries with my parcel company in seconds
- a **blog that drafts its own articles** three times a week, each of which I review and approve before anything is published
- a **video advertisement factory** that turns simple designs into short advertisements in Khmer and English
- an **ebook system** that helped me plan, research and write the book you are now reading

All four were built through conversation with an AI. I described what I needed in plain language, the AI built it, I tested it, and together we corrected whatever went wrong. The tools behind all four projects cost me about $31 a month.

This book tells that story honestly, from the very beginning.

### Why I wrote this book

There is no shortage of books about AI. Most of them make a promise: easy money, a hundred clever prompts, a business that runs while you sleep. When I studied them on Amazon, I noticed something missing. Very few were written by an ordinary person sharing real projects, with real costs and real mistakes.

That is what I can offer. I am a former research supervisor who left a difficult job, started a small delivery business, and chose to learn AI by using it every day. I am still learning. Some of my projects have only just begun, and I will tell you exactly where each one stands, including the parts that have not yet worked.

### Who this book is for

This book is written for you if:

- you run a small business, or hope to start one, and have never written a line of code
- you have heard that AI can do remarkable things, but are unsure where to begin
- you want to understand the risks of AI as well as its promise
- English is not your first language, and you have wondered whether AI is really meant for people like you

It is probably not the right book if you are searching for a quick route to wealth, or if you are already an experienced software developer. You will find no promises of income here, and no complicated code.

### What you will find inside

**Part One, *Why I Started*,** tells my own story: the job that taught me why reliable data and honest decisions matter, the warnings now emerging from inside the AI industry, the widening divide between those who use AI and those left behind by it, and the reasons I chose to build four real projects.

**Part Two, *Building*,** forms the heart of the book. It sets out what my tools cost, how I learned to talk to AI so that it builds what I actually need, and how you can build your own first small tool in about thirty minutes. It then tells the story of each of my four projects: the problem, the decisions and the reasoning behind them, what went wrong, and what happened next.

**Part Three, *What Comes Next*,** draws together the hardest lessons I learned: about AI's mistakes, about keeping your business and your customers safe, and about the true cost of building, which is time. It closes with a simple seven-day plan to help you begin your own first project.

### How to read this book

Each chapter in Part Two tells a story first and explains the reasoning behind every decision. If you would like to build something yourself, look for the boxes titled **Builder's Notes** at the end of those chapters. They contain the practical steps, messages you can send to an AI, and the traps to avoid. If you are interested only in the ideas, you can skip them without losing the thread of the story.

You need not read the book in order. If you are eager to begin, Chapters 6 and 7 will have you building quickly. I hope, however, that you will also read the stories around them, because the "why" behind each decision is often more valuable than the "how".

### A promise, and a confession

While writing this book, I made myself one promise: **nothing in it would be invented.** Every result, every figure and every story is real. Where a result does not yet exist, I say so. Where I quote someone, I have checked the source. Where I was wrong, I tell you.

Now the confession. This book was written with the help of AI, in the same way that my four projects were built. I shared my notes, memories and opinions, sometimes in imperfect English and sometimes mixed with Khmer. The AI helped me shape them into clear chapters, checked facts and sources, and told me whenever something could not be verified. I then read every chapter, corrected it and revised it until it said what I meant. The experience, the decisions and the mistakes are all my own. In Chapter 11, I explain exactly how we worked together.

That seems fitting. This is a book about what one person can build with AI, and the book itself is one of those creations.

### Before we begin

When I set out on this journey, I asked myself a simple question: *if AI is going to change the way we all live and work, am I ready?* At the start, my honest answer was no. Today, I am far more ready than I was, not because I understand everything, but because I have begun.

I hope this book helps you begin too.
