# Introduction

I am not a programmer. I studied computer science at university, but my major was networking, not building software. Until recently, I had never built an app, run a website or edited a video.

But in a few weeks, working from Phnom Penh, I built four real tools for my business and my writing:

- a **booking app** inside Telegram, so online sellers can book a delivery with my parcel company in seconds
- a **blog that drafts its own articles** three times a week, and I check every one before it goes live
- a **video advertisement factory** that turns simple designs into short advertisements in Khmer and English
- an **ebook system** that helped me plan, research and write the book you are now reading

I built all four by talking with an AI. I explained what I needed in plain words, the AI built it, I tested it, and together we fixed what went wrong. The tools behind all four projects cost me about $31 a month.

This book tells that story honestly, from the very beginning.

### Why I wrote this book

There are many books about AI. Most of them promise something: easy money, a hundred clever prompts, a business that runs while you sleep. When I looked at them on Amazon, I saw something missing. Very few were written by an ordinary person sharing real projects, with real costs and real mistakes.

That is what I can offer. I studied computer science, worked for years as a researcher, left a difficult job, started a small delivery business, and decided to learn AI by using it every day. I am still learning. Some of my projects have only just begun, and I will tell you exactly where each one stands, including the parts that have not yet worked.

### Who this book is for

This book is written for you if:

- you run a small business, or hope to start one, and have never written a line of code
- you have heard that AI can do amazing things, but you don't know where to begin
- you want to understand the risks of AI as well as its promise
- English is not your first language, and you have wondered whether AI is really meant for people like you

This is probably not your book if you want to get rich quickly, or if you are already an experienced programmer. There are no income promises here, and no complicated code.

### What you will find inside

**Part One, *Why I Started*,** tells my own story: the job that taught me why reliable data and honest decisions matter, the warnings now emerging from inside the AI industry, the widening divide between those who use AI and those left behind by it, and the reasons I chose to build four real projects.

**Part Two, *Building*,** is the heart of the book. It shows what my tools cost, how I learned to talk to AI so that it builds what I actually need, and how you can build your own first small tool in about thirty minutes. It then tells the story of each of my four projects: the problem, the decisions and the reasoning behind them, what went wrong, and what happened next.

**Part Three, *What Comes Next*,** collects the hardest lessons I learned: about AI's mistakes, about keeping your business and your customers safe, and about the true cost of building, which is time. It closes with a simple seven-day plan to help you begin your own first project.

### How to read this book

Each chapter in Part Two tells a story first and explains the reasoning behind every decision. If you would like to build something yourself, look for the boxes titled **Builder's Notes** at the end of those chapters. They contain the practical steps, messages you can send to an AI, and the traps to avoid. If you only want the ideas, you can skip them and still follow the story.

You don't have to read the book in order. If you want to start now, go to Chapters 6 and 7. But I hope you will also read the stories around them, because the "why" behind each decision is often worth more than the "how".

### A promise, and a confession

While writing this book, I made myself one promise: **nothing in it would be invented.** Every result, every figure and every story is real. Where a result does not yet exist, I say so. Where I quote someone, I have checked the source. Where I was wrong, I tell you.

Now the confession. This book was written with the help of AI, in the same way that my four projects were built. I shared my notes, memories and opinions, sometimes in imperfect English and sometimes mixed with Khmer. The AI helped me turn them into clear chapters, checked facts and sources, and told me when something could not be checked. Then I read every chapter and corrected it until it said what I meant. The experience, the decisions and the mistakes are all my own. In Chapter 11, I explain exactly how we worked together.

I think that is fair. This is a book about what one person can build with AI, and the book is one of those things.

### Before we begin

When I set out on this journey, I asked myself a simple question: *if AI is going to change the way we all live and work, am I ready?* At the start, my honest answer was no. Today, I am much more ready, not because I understand everything, but because I have started.

I hope this book helps you begin too.
