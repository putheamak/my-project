# Launch promotion plan

For *Built with AI, No Coding*, by Mak Puthea. The book went live on Amazon on 12 November 2026 (Kindle, $2.99, KDP Select).
Replace **[BOOK LINK]** everywhere with the book's Amazon link (from the "Live" email, or search the title in the Kindle Store and copy the page address).

## Rules (keep the account safe)
- Ask for **honest** reviews only. Never pay, never give gifts or free products for a review, never ask for 5 stars, never swap reviews with other authors.
- Family members and close friends should **not** review the book (Amazon removes reviews from people with a close personal relationship to the author).
- Do not buy your own book through your own affiliate links. Do not buy and return copies to push the ranking.
- Free Book Promotion days are allowed (they are Amazon's own tool).

| 7 | Check **KDP → Reports**: sales, Kindle Unlimited pages read. Note the numbers in the weekly tracker. |

## Next 3 weeks
- **Free Book Promotion (KDP Select):** up to 5 free days in each 90-day period. Suggestion: 2 days, about 10 days after launch, announced on Facebook, LinkedIn and Telegram the day before. Free downloads bring readers and, later, honest reviews. KDP → Bookshelf → "…" → KDP Select → Free Book Promotion.
- **After 2–3 weeks:** raise the price to **$4.99**.
- **Kindle Countdown Deal** (later): needs the same list price for 30 days before the deal, so use it only after the price has stayed at $4.99 for 30 days.
- Ask 2–3 local groups (universities, NGOs, business clubs) whether you can give a free 30-minute talk: "What I built with AI for $31 a month". Mention the book at the end.

## Author Central biography (paste in Author Central)
Mak Puthea is a researcher and small-business founder based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. A computer science graduate who majored in networking, he worked in qualitative research with social NGOs and later as a research supervisor in the private sector, where he taught himself to automate data collection, analysis and reporting. In October 2026 he launched Khmer Parcel, a parcel delivery service, and began building real business tools with AI: a Telegram booking app, a blog engine, a video ad factory and the system that helped him write his first book, *Built with AI, No Coding*. He writes simply and honestly about what AI can and cannot do for ordinary people, especially in developing countries.
