What does AI really cost a small business?

Not what the ads say.

My four tools — a Telegram booking app, a blog engine, a video-ad factory and a book-writing workflow — cost about $31 a month in subscriptions.

The real cost was my time: almost two months, 4 to 8 hours a day, describing problems, testing, fixing mistakes and learning how each system works.

And the results so far are honest, not glamorous:
📦 32 bookings through the app in our first month
📝 27 blog posts, 58 real visits in 12 days — and 0 sales
🎬 2,546 ad views — and 0 bookings from our first paid test

I wrote the full story, with the five lessons I learned, on Medium. Link in the first comment 👇
