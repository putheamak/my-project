# LinkedIn post 2 — "My AI wanted to make promises I never made"

*Post next week, on a weekday, around 8–10 pm Cambodia time. Use `linkedin-cover-1080x1350.jpg` as the image (or no image).*

---

My AI wanted to promise my customers things my business had never offered.

When I asked it for advertising ideas for my new delivery business in Phnom Penh, the first drafts were full of attractive promises: same-day delivery, same-day cash-on-delivery payouts.

They sounded great. I had not decided to offer either of them.

A young business that breaks one promise can lose its reputation in a week. So I stopped and wrote a short rules file that the AI now reads before every task:

✅ Never invent prices, offers or promises — ask me first.
✅ Use only our official phone numbers, exactly as written.
✅ Write customer messages in Khmer first, with short English below.
✅ One type of customer per ad.

It took 10 minutes. It has saved me from mistakes every week since.

AI is designed to be helpful — sometimes too helpful. It does not know which promises your business can keep. You do.

If you use AI for your business, what is the one rule you would give it first? 👇
