2,546 views. 57 clicks. 0 bookings.

That was the result of the first paid video ads for my new delivery business in Phnom Penh. Two days, $5 a day.

I was disappointed. Then I did what my years as a researcher taught me: I treated the numbers as research findings, and I showed them to an AI.

Three things stood out:

1️⃣ The average viewer watched for 4 seconds.
→ The first frame is everything. Now every video starts with the message already on screen.

2️⃣ Viewers left at second 17 — exactly when the ad switched from one type of customer to another.
→ One ad, one audience. A video that speaks to two groups loses both.

3️⃣ 4 people applied to become riders.
→ People were watching. Just not the people I expected.

The zero did not stay a zero. By the end of October, 32 parcels had been booked through our Telegram booking app — a tool I built with AI, although I am not a programmer.

The lesson I keep learning: AI is fast at making things. But only a person who looks honestly at the numbers can tell you what they mean.
