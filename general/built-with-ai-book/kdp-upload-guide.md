# Uploading the book to Amazon KDP: step by step

For *Built with AI, No Coding*, by Mak Puthea. Written 4 November 2026. Amazon sometimes moves buttons or renames them; the order stays the same.
Have these two files ready on your computer:
- **Book file:** `built-with-ai-no-coding.epub`
- **Cover file:** `cover-kdp-1600x2560.jpg`

Copy-and-paste text comes from `launch-kit-draft.md` (version 3).

## Before you start (once)
1. Sign in at **kdp.amazon.com** with your Amazon account.
2. **Account → Getting Paid:** add how you will be paid. **Account → Tax information:** complete the tax interview with your real details. Cambodia has no tax treaty with the US, so 30% of US royalties will be withheld. KDP will not let you publish until both are done.

## Step 1: Create the book
**Bookshelf → + Create → Kindle eBook.**

## Step 2: Kindle eBook Details (first page)
| Box | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Language | English |
| Book title | Built with AI, No Coding |
| Subtitle | Real Business Tools, Real Costs and Real Mistakes from a Small-Business Owner in Cambodia |
| Series / Edition number | Leave empty (or Edition 1) |
| Author | First name **Mak**, last name **Puthea** (exactly as on the cover) |
| Contributors | Leave empty |
| Description | Paste the description from the launch kit, section 2. Use the bold and bullet buttons in the box so it looks like the launch kit. |
| Publishing rights | **I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights** |
| Primary audience | Sexually explicit: **No**. Reading age: leave empty |
| Primary marketplace | **Amazon.com** |
| Categories | Click **Choose categories** and pick the three from the launch kit, section 4 |
| Keywords | Paste the 7 keywords from the launch kit, section 3, one per box |
| Release option | **I am ready to release my book now** |

Click **Save and Continue**.

## Step 3: Kindle eBook Content (second page)
| Box | What to choose |
|---|---|
| Digital Rights Management (DRM) | **Yes** (protects the file from copying). This cannot be changed later. |
| Manuscript | **Upload manuscript** → choose `built-with-ai-no-coding.epub` |
| Kindle eBook Cover | **Upload a cover you already have** → choose `cover-kdp-1600x2560.jpg` |
| AI-generated content | **Yes**. Text: the AI drafted the chapters from your notes and you edited every chapter, so choose the answer for the **entire work, with extensive editing**, and name the tool: **Claude (Anthropic)**. Images: answer **Yes** only if an AI tool made or "enhanced" the cover, and name that tool. |
| Kindle eBook Preview | Click **Launch Previewer**. Check the cover, the Contents, a few note links, and the Khmer text. |
| ISBN | Leave empty (Kindle books get a free ASIN) |

Click **Save and Continue**.

## Step 4: Kindle eBook Pricing (third page)
| Box | What to choose |
|---|---|
| KDP Select | **Enroll** (Kindle Unlimited; you are paid per page read; the ebook must be sold only on Amazon for 90 days) |
| Territories | **All territories (worldwide rights)** |
| Primary marketplace | Amazon.com |
| Royalty plan | **70%** |
| Price | **$2.99** (Amazon fills in the other countries; you can leave them) |
| Book Lending | Leave ticked |

Tick that you agree to the terms, then click **Publish Your Kindle eBook**.

## After you click Publish
- Amazon reviews the book in **up to 72 hours**. You will get an email when it is live.
- If Amazon asks a question or rejects something, forward the email text (no passwords) and we will fix it.
- When it is live: share the link with your network, and ask early readers for honest reviews. Never pay for reviews, and never ask friends to write fake ones.
- After **2–3 weeks**, change the price to **$4.99**: Bookshelf → "…" next to the book → **Edit eBook pricing**.
