## 2. Must-fix before publishing

### M1. Chapter 1: former employer (Cambodian criminal defamation and confidentiality risk)

The company is not named. But "research supervisor, three years, one of the leading consumer brands in its industry, left 20 August 2026" (repeated in About the Author) makes it identifiable to anyone in your industry, and to anyone who has seen your LinkedIn. Three sentences go beyond your own experience. They accuse identifiable people of unethical conduct, or they disclose the company's internal research findings. Opinion framing ("in my view") does not protect you reliably under Cambodia's Criminal Code (Arts. 305 and 307). Soften them. The story loses nothing.

| Quote (exact) | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "New people were hired under conditions that, in my view, raised serious questions of ethics and professionalism. Some colleagues were pushed out." | "New people were hired, and some colleagues left, not always by choice." |
| "New employees seemed to be judged less on their work than on whose side they were on, and those who did not fit in found it very hard to stay." | "It often felt that fitting in mattered as much as the work itself, and many people found it hard to settle." |
| "But our research showed clearly that it was not as strong as it had been. On the measures that matter most, being the first brand people think of (**top of mind**), **brand share**, and **market position**, the main competitors were getting closer every year." | "But our research suggested that its lead was getting smaller on the measures that matter most, such as being the first brand people think of (**top of mind**) and **brand share**." |

Optional: also change the heading "Inside a company that was losing its way" to "Inside a large company".

### M2. Chapter 9: Amazon click numbers contradict the box

The text says: "Amazon's dashboard recorded a few dozen clicks on product links in the first month, many of them my own tests." The box says: "Amazon counted 120 in October." The project notes show 69 clicks by 16 October, so both figures are real, but a reader sees a contradiction.

**Replace:** "Amazon's dashboard recorded a few dozen clicks on product links in the first month, many of them my own tests."
**With:** "By mid-October, Amazon's dashboard had recorded about 70 clicks on product links, most of them my own tests."

### M3. Chapter 9: "After two months" is wrong

The first post went up on 26 September, and you joined Associates on 1 October. On 31 October that is about five weeks.

**Replace:** "After two months, this is my honest conclusion."
**With:** "After my first five weeks, this is my honest conclusion."

### M4. Chapter 9: wrong cross-reference to Chapter 4

The text says: "In Chapter 4, I explained why I chose **affiliate marketing** and a blog about **kitchen gadgets under $30**: no stock, no shipping, free to join, a subject that never goes out of date, and products people buy without thinking for long." Chapter 4 gives none of these reasons. It only says you wanted to test the "passive income" promise.

**Replace with:** "I chose **affiliate marketing** and a blog about **kitchen gadgets under $30** for simple reasons: no stock, no shipping, free to join, a subject that never goes out of date, and products people buy without thinking for long."

### M5. Chapter 12 contradicts Chapter 10 on videos

Ch 12 says: "The booking app has only just launched, the blog has no sale yet, and most videos in the calendar are still to be made." Ch 10 says 17 Reels were posted by 31 October, from a 30-day calendar.

**Replace with:** "The booking app has only just launched, the blog has no sale yet, and the video ads have not brought a booking yet."

### M6. Note 3-4 points to the wrong note

The note reads: "Stanford HAI, *AI Index Report 2026* (see note 8), chapter on the economy." Note 3-8 is the PrahokBART paper. The AI Index is Chapter 2, note 5.

**Replace with:** "Stanford HAI, *AI Index Report 2026* (see Chapter 2, note 5), chapter on the economy."

### M7. Name the blog correctly

The book says "**Home Picks Under 30**" twice (Ch 4 and Ch 9). The live site, TikTok and YouTube all use **"Home Picks Under $30"** (`site.json`). A reader who searches for the name in the book should find the site.

**Replace both with:** "**Home Picks Under $30**". The domain *homepicksunder30.com* stays as it is.

*(None of these is a KDP rejection risk. KDP will accept the file as it is, with a correct AI disclosure. M1 is a legal risk. M2–M7 are accuracy errors in a book whose whole promise is accuracy.)*

## 7. Launch kit feedback

- **Description.** It is accurate and honest. Three gaps:
  1. **It does not mention Part One at all.** Chapters 1–3 are about a quarter of the book (AI risk, the new divide, Cambodia). Buyers who expect a pure how-to may feel misled, and readers interested in AI and developing countries won't know the book is for them. Add a bullet under "Inside, you will learn": "**Why AI could widen the gap between rich and developing countries, and what a country like Cambodia can do**".
  2. "for about $31 a month" → "for about $31 a month in tool costs". Your own Ch 12 says the real cost is time.
  3. Use your best asset, the honest numbers. After the second paragraph, add: "Every project ends with a 'Where things stand' box with real figures, including the zeros: 2,546 ad views and no bookings, a blog with no sales yet."
- **Keywords.** They are compliant. "real AI projects and costs" and "artificial intelligence guide for non-programmers" are weak searches. Consider "build apps with AI no programming", "vibe coding for beginners" and "AI for entrepreneurs in Asia".
- **Categories.** Fine. Before choosing the third, check the rank of the #20 book in each candidate (the same method as Ch 11). "Business & Money › Entrepreneurship" or a business-technology subcategory may be easier to rank in than AI & Machine Learning.
- **Price.** $4.99 with KDP Select is sensible for about 26,000 words. Consider $2.99 for the first 2–3 weeks to collect reviews.
- **Tax and payment (not in the kit, but important).** Cambodia has **no income-tax treaty with the US**, so expect **30% US withholding** on US royalties after the KDP tax interview. The book's own Builder's Note 6 says "confirm how you will be paid in your country". Do this in KDP's payment settings now (EFT or wire to a Cambodian bank). Don't wait for the first sale.
- **Cover.** Make sure the exported 1600 × 2560 cover passes the thumbnail test. Declare AI use for the cover image if any AI "enhance" tool was used.
