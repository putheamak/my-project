# Next ebook: topic research (4 November 2026)

Goal (owner): a topic the whole world is talking about, linked to daily life in every country, and built on the owner's real experience in Cambodia.

## What the world is worried about (2026 surveys)
- Cost of living is the world's top concern for the fifth year running (FII PRIORITY Compass).
- In 34 of 37 countries, more people expect AI to cut jobs than to create them (Pew, Feb–May 2026). Ipsos: 67% expect many jobs lost to AI in their country.
- 61-country survey (about 60,000 adults, Oct 2025 – Jan 2026): 36% worried AI could replace their job; worry is higher in fast-growing developing economies (e.g. India 75% "definitely" or "probably").
- AI scams: the FBI's 2025 report added AI-enabled scams for the first time ($893 million reported lost in the US).
- Side income: in the US, 32% have a side hustle and 30% plan to start one in 2026 (Monster).

## Shortlist (ranked)
1. **"Will AI Take My Job?" — what workers in Cambodia told me, and what every worker can do next.**
   The owner is a trained qualitative researcher: interview 20–30 real workers (garment, office, riders, sellers, students), report their words and fears, add the facts from Book 1 Ch 2–3, and give a practical plan for workers anywhere. Original research = the difference from the many generic "AI and jobs" books. About 3–4 months (interviews need consent and anonymity).
2. **"From Job to Business in the AI Age" — the first six months of a delivery start-up in Phnom Penh.**
   A build journal of Khmer Parcel: costs, customers, riders, mistakes, the AI tools. Everyone uses deliveries and online shopping. Needs results first, so it fits mid-2027.
3. **AI scams and ordinary families.** Hot everywhere, but sensitive for an author in Cambodia (scam compounds) and less of the owner's own experience. Not recommended as a full book.
4. **Cashless: what Cambodia's QR payments (KHQR, Bakong) teach the world.** Real daily experience, but a small market.
