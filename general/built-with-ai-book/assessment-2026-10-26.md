## Top fixes, in order
1. Add a short Introduction (1–2 pages): who the book is for, what readers will be able to do, what the four projects are.
   Amazon's "Look Inside" shows roughly the first 10%; today that is Chapter 1 (old job) and Chapter 2 (AI risk),
   about 2,400 words before any building.
2. Bring back the author's own voice: some of the formal polish ("precisely" ×9, "genuinely" ×7) can be simplified,
   and a few of the owner's original phrases can return. Chapter 12 itself says "keep your own voice".
3. Reduce repeated patterns: "The principle:" appears 13 times and "Why it matters:" 12 times. Keep the best ones.
4. Choose US or British spelling. The book uses British spelling (organisation, colour, programme, analyse) while
   the main market is the US. Recommend US spelling for Amazon.com readers.
5. Add screenshots (booking app, blog, pin page, a video frame, tracker), with customer data hidden.
6. Add a short glossary (API, bot, token, KV, GitHub, affiliate, KDP, BSR).
7. Fill the ⭐ gaps with real tracker numbers; publish with at least one real result if possible.
8. Front and back matter: title page, copyright page, AI-use statement, About the Author, a request for an honest review.
9. Consider shortening Chapters 2–3 (world affairs and wars date quickly) or moving some of it later.

## Tools to assess the book
Before publishing:
- ProWritingAid or Grammarly: grammar, style, repeated words, readability reports.
- Hemingway Editor: readability grade (aim for about grade 8–9 for a global audience).
- Beta readers: 3–5 people from the target audience (small-business owners, non-programmers). Ask what was confusing,
  boring or most useful.
- Professional editorial assessment (optional, paid): editors on Reedsy.
- Kindle Previewer (free, from Amazon): checks how the book looks on Kindle devices and phones.

After publishing:
- KDP Reports: sales, and KENP pages read (Kindle Unlimited), which shows whether people finish the book.
- Amazon Best Sellers Rank, star rating and written reviews.
- Goodreads ratings and reviews.
- Optional paid tools: Publisher Rocket (keywords and categories).
- Early reviews: Amazon allows giving free copies of books to readers, as long as no review (or positive review)
  is required in return. Never pay for or swap reviews.
