# Launch Kit

**Built with AI, No Coding**, by Mak Puthea
Version 2, 26 October 2026. Decisions approved by the author, who accepted all recommendations.

---

## 1. Title and subtitle (approved)

> **Built with AI, No Coding**
> *Real Business Tools, Real Costs and Real Mistakes from a Small-Business Owner in Cambodia*

**Why this title works.** The title states plainly what the book delivers. The subtitle explains what sets it apart, namely real projects, honestly reported, by a real business owner, while carrying the terms buyers search for: *business tools*, *costs* and *small-business owner*. Together they come to about 120 characters, comfortably within KDP's 200-character limit.

**Options considered and set aside:**
- *Built with AI, No Coding: How a Small-Business Owner in Cambodia Built a Booking App, a Self-Drafting Blog, Video Ads and an Ebook Machine*: concrete, but long and less memorable.
- *I'm Not a Programmer: How I Built Real Business Tools with AI for About $31 a Month*: personal and intriguing, but it ties the book to a price that may change.
- *AI for the Rest of Us*: warm and inclusive, but already a common phrase.
- *The Honest AI Builder*: distinctive, but says less about what the reader will gain.

## 6. Cover (approved 27 Oct 2026: `cover/cover-draft.jpg`, built from the brief below)

### Cover brief

**Format:** 1,600 × 2,560 pixels (KDP's recommended 1.6 ratio), JPG, RGB colour.

**Cover text:**
- Title: **BUILT WITH AI, NO CODING**, large and bold, legible at thumbnail size
- Cover line: *Real business tools, real costs, real mistakes*
- Author: **MAK PUTHEA**

**Design direction:**
- Clean, modern and confident. Avoid robots, glowing brains and images of money, the visual language of the hype titles this book stands apart from.
- Central idea: **four simple icons** representing the four projects (a booking form, a blog page, a play button and a book), connected like steps or arranged around a phone or laptop.
- Palette: one strong colour with generous white space, such as deep blue with orange accents, to stand out against the dark and gold covers common in this category.
- **No third-party logos** (Telegram, Claude, Amazon or others).

**Author photo:** best placed inside the book, in About the Author, rather than on the cover. Successful non-fiction covers in this category rely on typography and simple graphics.

**Final check:** reduce the cover to thumbnail size on a phone screen. If the title remains easy to read, the cover is ready.
