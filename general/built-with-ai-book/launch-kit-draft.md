## 1. Title and subtitle options

KDP allows a title and subtitle of up to 200 characters in total. The subtitle carries search words, so it can be longer than what appears on the cover.

| B | Built with AI, No Coding | How a Small-Business Owner in Cambodia Built a Booking App, a Self-Drafting Blog, Video Ads and an Ebook Machine | Very concrete; shows exactly what readers get (current working subtitle) |
| C | I'm Not a Programmer | How I Built Real Business Tools with AI for About $31 a Month | Personal and curious; the price is a strong hook |
| D | AI for the Rest of Us | Building Real Business Tools Without Coding, from a Small-Business Owner in Cambodia | Inclusive and warm; speaks to beginners worldwide |
| E | The Honest AI Builder | Four Real Projects, $31 a Month, and No Coding | Stands out against "Bible" and "Millionaire" titles |

**My recommendation: A.** The title says what the book is; the subtitle says why it is different (real, honest, from a real person) and contains search words such as "business tools", "costs" and "small-business owner".

## 5. Price and programme (suggestion)

- **Price:** $4.99 at launch, then $6.99. KDP pays the 70% royalty rate for prices between $2.99 and $9.99.
- **Kindle Unlimited (KDP Select):** recommended. Most competing books are in it, and you are paid for pages read. It requires the ebook to be sold only on Amazon for 90 days at a time.

## 6. Cover brief (for Canva or a designer)

**Size:** 1,600 × 2,560 pixels (KDP's recommended ratio of 1.6), JPG, RGB colour.

**Text on the cover:**
- Title: **BUILT WITH AI, NO CODING** (large, bold, readable as a small thumbnail)
- Short cover subtitle: *Real business tools, real costs and real mistakes*
- Author: **MAK PUTHEA**

**Style:**
- Clean, modern and confident; not a robot, not a glowing brain, not a "money" image. Those are what the hype books use.
- A simple visual idea: **four small icons** for the four projects (a booking form, a blog page, a play button, a book), connected like steps or arranged around a phone or laptop.
- Colours: one strong colour with plenty of white space, for example deep blue with orange accents, so it stands out in Amazon's mostly dark, gold AI covers.
- **Do not use other companies' logos** (Telegram, Claude, Amazon, etc.) on the cover.

**Photo:** optional. Your author photo works better inside the book (About the Author) than on the cover; most successful non-fiction covers in this niche use type and simple graphics.

**Test before you choose:** shrink the cover to the size of a thumbnail on your phone. If you can still read the title, it works.
