### Frequently asked questions

#### What if the dorm doesn't allow small appliances?

Many dorms restrict anything with a heating element. When in doubt, go microwave-only: the ramen cooker, the pasta bowl, the egg cooker, the sandwich maker and the silicone popcorn popper all run in a shared microwave.

#### Can I combine a few of these into one gift?

Yes, and it's usually the better move. A pan plus a spatula set, or a rice cooker plus a bowl, reads as a thought-out starter kit rather than a single gadget. Just check the current prices on Amazon so the total still fits your budget.

#### What's a safe pick if I don't know their kitchen at all?

The microwave sandwich maker or the personal blender. Both work in almost any living situation and don't duplicate something they probably already own.

#### Are these good for a first apartment, not just a dorm?

Absolutely. Most of this list is really "small kitchen" gear - the same picks work for a recent grad, a studio apartment, or anyone cooking for one.
