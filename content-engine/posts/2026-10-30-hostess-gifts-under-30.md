### Frequently asked questions

#### Is a kitchen gadget too personal for a hostess gift?
Not if you stick to neutral, universal tools - a zester, a butter dish, a white gravy boat. Save the strongly styled items for people whose kitchens you've actually stood in.

#### What's a good last-minute hostess gift?
Anything that doesn't need sizing or guessing: a milk frother, a popcorn popper with seasonings, or an apron-and-mitts bundle. Check delivery dates before you order.

#### Should I bring a hostess gift if I'm already bringing a dish?
A dish counts. If you still want to bring something, keep it small - a set of coffee scoops or a zester tucked in a gift bag is plenty.

#### What if the host has a tiny kitchen?
Choose flat or collapsible items: the silicone popcorn popper, herb scissors, the pie server, or the apron. Skip anything with a bulky footprint.
