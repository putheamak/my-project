### Try this tonight: a simple cheese board in 10 minutes

1. Pick three cheeses that feel different: one soft (like brie), one hard (like aged cheddar) and one with a strong flavor (like blue cheese). Take them out of the fridge 30 to 60 minutes before guests arrive, so they taste of more.
2. Add something crunchy (crackers or nuts) and something sweet (grapes, apple slices or a spoon of jam) in small piles around them.
3. Give each cheese its own knife, so the blue cheese doesn't end up on the brie.

Step three is why a cheese knife set makes such a good hostess gift: it gets used the moment the board goes out.
