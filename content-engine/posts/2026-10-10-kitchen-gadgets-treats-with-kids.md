#### Do I need an oven for any of this?

No. The donut maker, waffle maker, popcorn makers, cotton candy maker and frother all work without one. Only the donut pan and the ramekins need an oven.

#### Which of these are safe for kids to operate themselves?

Treat all heating appliances as adult-run. Kids can measure, pour, decorate and shake seasoning. The cotton candy maker in particular should be run by an adult.

#### How do I store all of this in a small kitchen?

Pick the collapsible and stackable options first — the silicone popper folds flat, the ramekins nest, and the mini makers are roughly the footprint of a coffee mug.
