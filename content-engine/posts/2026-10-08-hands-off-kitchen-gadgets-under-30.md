### What makes a kitchen gadget actually "hands-off"?

Three things:

- **It stops or signals on its own**, or it runs on a timer you set once (microwave, cooker switch).
- **It contains the mess**, so there's no splatter to watch and no pan to scrub mid-cook.
- **It doesn't need stirring.** Anything that requires a stir every two minutes isn't hands-off, it's just a different kind of chore.

Everything here fits at least two of those.

#### Do I still have to watch microwave gadgets?

Not really — you set the time and the microwave stops itself. The thing to watch is the *first* run: start on the shorter end of the suggested time, check, then add seconds. Once you know the right time for your microwave, you can repeat it.
