### Frequently asked questions

#### Is it safe to leave a slow cooker on while I'm at work?

Slow cookers are designed to run unattended for hours on low. Set it on a clear counter away from anything that can catch heat, and follow its manual.

#### Do I need both a rice cooker and a slow cooker?

No. Get the rice cooker if your problem is fast weeknight sides and lunches. Get the slow cooker if your problem is having dinner ready when you walk in the door.

#### Is a 1.5-quart slow cooker big enough?

It suits one or two people, or a side dish and dip for a few more. If you regularly cook for four or more, a larger slow cooker will serve you better.
