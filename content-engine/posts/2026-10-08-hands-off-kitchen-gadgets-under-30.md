### Try this tonight: rinse and rest your rice

1. Put the rice in a bowl, cover it with cold water, swirl with your hand and pour off the cloudy water. Do it two or three times until the water looks mostly clear.
2. Drain well, then add fresh water following your cooker's measuring lines or the rice package. Rinsing washes off loose starch, which is what makes rice gummy.
3. When it's done, leave the lid closed for about 10 minutes before fluffing with a fork. The grains firm up and stop sticking to the paddle.

It works in a plain pot too, but a rice cooker lets you skip the timer-watching entirely.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I leave a slow cooker on while I'm at work?

Check the manual first; it says how the maker wants it used. A manual model like the bella doesn't switch itself off, so cook for the time your recipe gives and turn it off when it's done. Always set it on a clear counter away from anything that can catch heat.

#### Do I need both a rice cooker and a slow cooker?

No. Get the rice cooker if your problem is fast weeknight sides and lunches. Get the slow cooker if you want a small stew or chili that cooks on its own while you're home doing other things.

#### Is a 1.5-quart slow cooker big enough?

It suits one or two people, or a side dish and dip for a few more. If you regularly cook for four or more, a larger slow cooker will serve you better.
