### Try this tonight: prep everything before you turn on the heat

1. Read the recipe once, all the way through, before you touch a pan.
2. Chop, measure and open everything first, and set each item in its own small bowl or on a plate in the order you'll use it.
3. Only then turn on the burner. Cooking becomes a few calm minutes of adding things in order, with nothing burning while you hunt for the garlic.

Chefs call this "mise en place". The chopping in step two is where most people give up, and it's the job the pull chopper below is built for.
