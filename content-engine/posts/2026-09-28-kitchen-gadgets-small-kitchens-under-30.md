### Try this tonight: save a mug of pasta water

1. Right before the pasta is done, dip a mug into the pot and set aside about a cup of the cloudy cooking water.
2. Drain the pasta and tip it back into the pot or pan with your sauce.
3. Splash in some of that water and toss over low heat for a minute. The starch helps the sauce cling instead of pooling, so even jarred sauce tastes more finished.

Draining is what eats counter space, which is where the first pick comes in.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will an over-sink colander or roll-up rack fit my sink?

Measure the inside width of your sink and compare it to the size shown in the listing. Rental sinks vary a lot, so check before you order.

#### Are flexible cutting mats safe for knives and raw meat?

They work with regular kitchen knives, and having several mats lets you keep raw meat away from produce. Wash the meat mat in hot, soapy water right after use, and replace any mat once it gets deeply scored.

#### Do I need an immersion blender if I already own a blender?

If your blender is easy to reach, probably not. If it sits behind other things in a cabinet, a stick blender in the drawer is the one you'll actually use.
