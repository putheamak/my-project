If you cook in a galley kitchen, a rental with three drawers, or a corner of a studio apartment, the problem usually isn't skill — it's square footage. ### What makes a gadget worth space in a small kitchen?

Before you buy anything, run it through four questions:

- **Does it nest, fold, or hang?** Collapsible and stackable designs are the difference between a full drawer and a half-empty one.
- **Does it do more than one job?** A tool with two real uses earns its spot; a tool with one novelty use doesn't.
- **Does it create dishes or remove them?** Anything that lets you prep and serve in the same vessel is a win.
- **Can you clean it in a small sink?** Wide, rigid, awkward items are miserable in an apartment sink.

If a gadget fails two of these, skip it — no matter how clever the demo video looks.

### How do these gadgets cut down on dishes?

The fewer vessels a recipe touches, the faster cleanup goes. Three habits do most of the work:

1. **Prep onto the mat, not into a bowl.** Flexible cutting mats bend into a chute — no prep bowls at all.
2. **Cook and store in the same container.** Bowls with lids skip the "transfer to a container" step entirely.
3. **Blend in the pot.** An immersion blender means one wand and one pot, not a blender base, jar, lid, and gasket.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What should I buy first if I only have one drawer?

Start with a flexible cutting mat set and one nesting bowl set. Those two cover prep, mixing, and storage — the three things you do every single day — and they take up almost no depth.

#### Are collapsible gadgets durable enough for daily use?

It depends on the build. Look for a solid rim or frame rather than all-silicone, check the manufacturer's listed heat limits, and read the product description for dishwasher guidance before you buy.

#### Do I need an immersion blender if I already own a blender?

If your countertop blender lives in a cabinet you have to unload to reach, yes — the friction is what stops you from using it. A stick blender stores in a drawer and cleans in seconds.
