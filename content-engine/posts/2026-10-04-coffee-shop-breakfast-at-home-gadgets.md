### Try this tonight: jar-shaken milk foam

1. Pour milk into a clean jar with a tight lid, filling it no more than halfway so the foam has room to grow.
2. Close the lid and shake hard for 30 to 60 seconds, until the milk looks thick and roughly doubled.
3. Take the lid off and microwave the jar for about 30 seconds; the heat firms up the foam so you can spoon it onto coffee.

If you like the result but not the shaking, the [handheld milk frother](https://amzn.to/4hs2w08) does the same job right in a mug.
