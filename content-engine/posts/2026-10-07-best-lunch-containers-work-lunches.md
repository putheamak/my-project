### What do you actually need to pack lunch the night before?

Fewer things than you'd guess. A night-before lunch needs four jobs covered:

- **A container that keeps foods separate** so your salad doesn't drown and your rice doesn't go soggy.
- **Something insulated** for the trip from your fridge to your desk.
- **A way to carry something hot or soupy** if sandwiches get old.
- **Utensils you don't have to beg for** in the break room.

Everything else is optional. Start with those four and your lunch habit survives a bad week.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How long does a night-before lunch stay good?
Follow standard food-safety guidance and your own judgement: keep it refrigerated until you leave, use an insulated bag for the commute, and don't let cooked food sit at room temperature all morning. If your office has a fridge, use it.

#### Can I microwave meal prep containers?
Only if the manufacturer says so — check the product listing and the symbols on the container base before you heat anything. Stainless steel items like the salad bowl kit and the insulated jar never go in the microwave.

#### Is a 20L lunch bag too big for one person?
Not if you pack a drink, a main and a snack. A bag that's slightly too big closes easily; a bag that's slightly too small means you leave something behind. If you only ever carry one sandwich, size down.

#### Do I need a rice cooker to meal prep?
No, but it removes a decision. Batch-cooking a grain on Sunday or on a weeknight gives every container a base, and a cooker with a steaming basket handles the vegetable at the same time.
