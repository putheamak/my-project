Try this tonight: reheat rice so it's soft again

  1. Put the cold rice in a microwave-safe bowl and sprinkle about a tablespoon of water over each cup. Break up any hard clumps with a fork.
  2. Cover the bowl loosely so the steam stays in. An upside-down plate works; a microwave cover is easier.
  3. Heat in short bursts, stirring between them, until it's steaming hot all the way through.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need both glass and plastic containers?

Not at first. Start with glass if you eat leftovers at home; add the plastic set only if you carry lunch out. Check each listing for microwave, freezer and dishwasher guidance before you buy.

Does a microwave cover help with texture?

That's what it's designed for. A cover traps steam, which generally helps keep the surface of rice, pasta and roasted vegetables from drying out, and it keeps sauce from splattering the microwave.

How long can I keep leftovers in the fridge?

It depends on the food, so follow the guidance from your local food safety authority. Labeling the date on each container is the easiest way to stay on top of it.
